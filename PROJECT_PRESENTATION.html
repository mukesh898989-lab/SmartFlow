Challenges & Solutions

Challenge 1

Queue Ordering: Keep correct priority order when patients change

✅ Solution: Database queries sort by priority.order, then createdAt

Challenge 2

Real-time Sync: Multiple screens must show same queue instantly

✅ Solution: WebSocket STOMP server-side push

Challenge 3

Data Consistency: Prevent duplicate operations and race conditions

✅ Solution: Database transactions & pessimistic locking

Challenge 4

Security: Patient privacy while allowing public tracking

✅ Solution: Token-based access (read-only, public)
